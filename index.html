How to spot a fake job in Kenya

KaziScout only links to official websites. Before you pay or share your ID, check these warning signs:

  • They ask you to pay via M-Pesa to unlock a job
  • They use Gmail or WhatsApp only, with no official website
  • Salary is too high for no experience
  • They rush you - pay today or lose the slot

Rule: Real employers in Kenya never ask you to pay to get a job.
